Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

How much does nonprofit video production cost?

A single-subject testimonial video from Josh Soliz Media starts at $2,500 and typically runs $2,500–$3,500, including one shoot day, editing, and two final cuts (a hero cut and a social media edit). A multi-subject series of three testimonials typically runs $5,000–$8,000. Pricing scales with the number of shoot days, subjects, travel, and deliverables.

How long does a nonprofit video project take?

Most testimonial projects are delivered within 10 business days of the shoot. Larger projects, multi-subject series, brand films, or event coverage, take 15–20 business days. Rush delivery (5–7 business days) is available for an additional fee.

Do you work with churches and ministries?

Yes. Josh spent five years on the production staff at Fresh Life Church and currently produces content at REBOOT Recovery, a faith-based nonprofit. He works comfortably in church environments and understands ministry communication goals, approval processes, and the sensitivities involved in sharing congregation member stories.

Can you film multiple subjects in one day?

Yes. With proper scheduling, Josh can film 2–3 interview subjects in a single production day. He provides a pre-interview prep guide to each subject in advance, which keeps on-camera time efficient, typically 45–75 minutes per subject including setup.

Do you offer social media edits from the same footage?

Yes. Every project includes at least one social media cut alongside the hero edit. Additional platform-specific cuts (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook) are available as add-ons at $150–$300 per cut.
