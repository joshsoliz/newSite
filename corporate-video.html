Types of work

What Josh produces for corporate clients.

01

Conference & Event Coverage

Multi-day conferences, leadership summits, and training events, capturing keynote presentations, breakout sessions, speaker interviews, and hallway B-roll. Deliverables include an edited highlight reel and, optionally, full-length session recordings.

02

Brand Films

A 3–5 minute film that shows who your company is, what you do, and why it matters. Interview-driven or narrated, Josh will recommend a structure based on your message and audience.

03

Training & Internal Video

Procedure walkthroughs, employee onboarding content, and safety training video, produced for in-house use. They don't need to be cinematic. They need to be clear, well-lit, and easy to follow.

04

Event Highlight Reels

A 60–90 second summary of your event, launch party, annual conference, grand opening, team offsite, edited and delivered within 5–10 business days. Shareable on LinkedIn, YouTube, or embedded on your site.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Do you handle audio at conferences and events?

Yes. For conference and event work, Josh mixes from the house audio board when available, for stage presentations, and uses lavalier mics for interviews and smaller sessions. He brings his own audio kit as backup.

Can you film and deliver a highlight reel on the same day?

For events where same-day delivery is a priority, Josh can produce a rough highlight reel on-site, typically a 60–90 second cut. Full color-graded and music-licensed delivery follows within 5 business days. Confirm same-day delivery in your initial inquiry so he can plan accordingly.

Do you bring your own lighting and equipment?

Yes. Josh operates a full production rig, Panasonic G85 (primary) with SmallRig cinema cage, Tilta matte box, follow focus, V-mount power, and FEELWORLD monitor. Lighting and audio are included in every quote. He does not require a studio or venue-provided gear.

Can you produce video for use in paid advertising?

Yes. Brand film footage and testimonial content can be re-cut for pre-roll ads, LinkedIn video ads, or YouTube campaigns. Music licensing for ad use is a separate cost and will be noted in your quote. Josh can also produce vertical and square formats alongside the primary deliverable.
