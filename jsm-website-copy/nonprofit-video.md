Most testimonial projects are delivered within 10 business days of the shoot. Larger projects — multi-subject series, brand films, or event coverage — take 15–20 business days. Rush delivery (5–7 business days) is available for an additional fee.

**3. Do you work with churches and ministries?**
Yes. Josh spent five years on the production staff at Fresh Life Church and currently produces content at REBOOT Recovery, a faith-based nonprofit. Yes. Every project includes at least one social media cut alongside the hero edit. Additional platform-specific cuts (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook) are available as add-ons at $150–$300 per cut.
