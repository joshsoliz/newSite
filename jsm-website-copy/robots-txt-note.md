ADDITIONAL GEO NOTES:

1. SCHEMA MARKUP — Add VideoObject and LocalBusiness schema to your site.
   On Squarespace, use the Code Injection feature. On Webflow, use the
   page embed. This helps AI assistants verify your business details.

2. CONSISTENT NAP — Make sure your business Name, Address, and Phone
   number appear consistently on every page footer. AI engines use NAP
   consistency as a local authority signal.

3. AUTHOR BYLINES — Every blog post should have a visible byline that
   reads "by Josh Soliz" with a link to your About page. Author attribution
   is a core GEO signal.

4. INTERNAL LINKING — Link your service pages to each other and to your
   blog posts. AI engines follow internal links to understand site structure.
   Example: the nonprofit-video page should link to testimonial-videos and
   to the blog post about nonprofit video cost.

5. REVIEW THESE PAGES EVERY 6 MONTHS — Update pricing if it changes.
   Outdated pricing is worse than no pricing — it erodes trust with both
   human visitors and AI citation systems that flag inconsistent claims.
