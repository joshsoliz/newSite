## SERVICES

**Lower thirds**
Animated name/title overlays for interview subjects. Clean, branded, consistent across a series. Starting at $100–$150 per design (with multiple exports for each subject).

**Title cards and section headers**
Animated section titles for branded video — church message series, course modules, branded content, social campaigns.

**Animated intros and outros**
Branded 3–8 second open and close for your video series. Built once, reused across every episode.

**Social media graphics (animated)**
Motion-treated versions of static graphics for Instagram stories, LinkedIn carousels, and Facebook posts. These perform measurably better than still images in most feeds.

**Ad animations**
15–30 second animated ad spots for digital advertising — pre-roll, social, display. Text animation, product reveal, offer callout.

**Lyric videos**
Animated lyric videos for worship teams, artists, or church services — built in After Effects with expression-driven text animation. Josh has developed a painterly lyric video system using dynamic matte and gradient tools.

**Sermon series graphics packages**
Complete visual identity for a church message series: title treatment, lower third template, background loops, social assets. Scoped per series.

## TOOLS AND WORKFLOW

- **Adobe After Effects** — primary motion design tool for all animations
- **Adobe Premiere Pro** — finishing, color grade, and audio mixing
- **Midjourney** — AI image generation for concept art, texture elements, and background development
- **Kling** — AI-assisted video animation for select motion projects
- **After Effects expressions** — expression-driven animation for dynamic, data-linked motion (not just keyframe-by-keyframe)

The AI tools are used to expand creative options and speed up specific production steps — not to replace craft judgment. Every deliverable goes through the same review and revision process regardless of how it was produced.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**1. Can you match our existing brand guidelines?**
Yes. If you have a brand guide, logo files, and font assets, send them with your inquiry. Josh builds all motion graphics to match client brand specs — color, typography, and visual language. If you don't have a formal brand guide, he'll establish a consistent treatment based on your existing materials.

**2. Do you deliver editable After Effects project files?**
On request, yes — for an additional fee. Most clients receive finished rendered files (MP4, MOV, GIF). If you want source files for in-house editing later, specify that in your inquiry and Josh will quote accordingly.

**3. How long does a motion design project take?**
Simple deliverables (lower thirds, animated logo) take 3–5 business days. Sermon series graphic packages typically take 10–15 business days. Lyric videos are 7–12 business days depending on complexity. Rush delivery is available.

**4. Can motion graphics be added to a video project I'm already doing with you?**
Yes, and it's the most cost-effective way to get them. If you're already shooting a testimonial or brand film with Josh, adding a lower third package or branded intro/outro to the same project saves time and costs less than ordering standalone.
