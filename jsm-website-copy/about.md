## ANSWER-FIRST INTRO

Josh Soliz is a video producer and motion designer based in Clarksville, Tennessee. He works with nonprofits, churches, and businesses across Middle Tennessee — producing testimonial videos, brand films, event coverage, and motion graphics through his studio, Josh Soliz Media (JSM).

---

## BACKGROUND AND CREDENTIALS

Josh has been working in professional video production since 2019. He spent five years on the production staff at Fresh Life Church, where he handled filming, editing, and motion graphics for weekly services, events, and digital content.

Since 2024, he has served as Visual Content Creator at REBOOT Recovery — a faith-based nonprofit that provides trauma recovery programs for veterans, first responders, and their families. In that role, Josh produces the organization's testimonial series, event coverage, and social media content — not as an outside contractor, but as an embedded member of the team.

That context matters. Most videographers produce for nonprofits. Josh works inside one.

**Years of professional experience:** 6+
**Organizations served:** Fresh Life Church, REBOOT Recovery, SCADA Academy, Manna Church, and others
**Location:** Clarksville, TN — serving Nashville, Fort Campbell area, and Middle Tennessee

## WHAT MAKES JSM DIFFERENT

**Church and nonprofit background — not just client experience.**
Five years in church production and a current staff role at REBOOT Recovery means Josh understands the communication goals, approval processes, and budget realities of mission-driven organizations. He knows what the executive director needs from a donor video. He knows what the lead pastor is looking for in a sermon series opener.

**Motion design in-house.**
Josh builds motion graphics — title cards, lower thirds, animated logos, social graphics — in After Effects. Most videographers outsource this or skip it entirely. At JSM, it's included or available as an add-on to any video project.

**AI-assisted production workflow.**
Josh incorporates Midjourney and Kling into select projects for concept visualization and animated elements — used responsibly, where it improves the work. This keeps creative options wider and production costs competitive without compromising quality.
