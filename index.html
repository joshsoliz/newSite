About

Hey, I'm Josh.
Glad you're here.

I got into this entirely by accident. Started volunteering at my church and one thing kept leading to another: sound mixing, then video production, then photography, then motion design. Best accident of my life.

I'm a one-man shop, which means you're not getting handed off to an assistant. You get me, start to finish. And I know video is an investment, so when someone decides it's worth it, I take that seriously. Every project gets treated like it's the most important one on my plate. Because to me, it is.
