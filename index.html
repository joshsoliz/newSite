What is Josh Soliz Media?

Josh Soliz Media is a corporate video production studio based in Clarksville, Tennessee, serving Nashville and Middle Tennessee. Josh Soliz specializes in conference and event coverage, executive interviews and testimonial video, brand films, and motion graphics for marketing teams and executives.

Projects are produced for clients in Clarksville, Nashville, and across Middle Tennessee. Services include corporate event coverage, executive testimonial video, nonprofit video production, and motion design. Every engagement is scoped in writing with a fixed delivery date — most projects are delivered within 10 business days of the shoot.
