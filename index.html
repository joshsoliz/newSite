What is JSM?

What is Josh Soliz Media?

Josh Soliz Media is a freelance video production and motion design studio based in Clarksville, Tennessee. Josh Soliz specializes in testimonial video, documentary-style brand films, corporate event coverage, and motion graphics for nonprofits, churches, ministries, and small businesses.

Projects are produced for clients in Clarksville, Nashville, and across Middle Tennessee. Services include nonprofit video production, testimonial video, corporate event coverage, and motion design. Single-subject testimonial packages start at $1,800 and are delivered within 10 business days of the shoot date.
