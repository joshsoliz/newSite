Easter Lyric Video

Life Church · Walla Walla, WA

Easter is the biggest Sunday of the year for any church — the programming element has to carry that weight. This lyric video was built to do exactly that: kinetic type, illustrated backgrounds, and a piece that builds from the first word to a final, full-screen "Jesus."

The design language came from original illustrations by Michael Morris, and the animation was built to honor that work — letting the type and motion serve the imagery, not compete with it.

The Brief

Easter deserves a moment, not a slide

Life Church came to us wanting something that felt as significant as the day itself — a programming element that could open the service and set the tone before a word was spoken from the stage. The brief was clear: make it feel big, make it feel earned, and land on Jesus.

Working from Michael Morris's illustrations gave the piece a visual foundation that felt intentional and hand-crafted. The job of the animation was to let those images breathe while keeping the energy of the type moving forward.

The Work

Every frame building toward one word

The whole piece is structured as a build — colored backgrounds cycling through the illustrated scenes, kinetic type synced to the track, dynamics that shift when the music does. The final moment holds on "Jesus" in full-screen type because that's what the song is actually about, and the animation needed to say it like it meant it.
