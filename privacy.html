Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information joshsoliz.com ("the site") collects and how it is used. The site is operated by Josh Soliz Media, based in Clarksville, Tennessee.

Information you send me

If you use the contact form, I receive the details you enter: your name, email address, organization, project type and message. If you use the email box on the home page, I receive your email address. Forms are processed by Formspree and delivered to my inbox. I use this information only to reply to you and to discuss a possible project. I don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Analytics and cookies

The site uses Google Analytics and HubSpot to understand which pages are visited and how people find the site. Analytics cookies are only enabled if you choose Accept on the cookie notice. If you choose Decline, the HubSpot tracking script is not loaded and Google Analytics runs in a limited mode that doesn't set analytics cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Third-party services

  • Bunny.net hosts and streams the videos on the site. Your IP address and device information are processed to deliver the video.
  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typefaces and receives your IP address when it does.
  • Formspree processes contact form submissions.
  • Links to Instagram and LinkedIn lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

How long I keep information

I keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and to keep business records, then delete them. Analytics data is kept according to the settings of the analytics providers.

Your choices

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the personal information I have about you by emailing hello@joshsoliz.com. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

Children

The site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
